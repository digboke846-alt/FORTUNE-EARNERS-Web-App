Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. What Is Fortune Earners All About?

Fortune Earners is a digital web-earning platform that rewards users for completing simple online tasks, viewing sponsored advertisements, and referring new members.

2. How Do I Activate A Plan?

Log in to your account, go to "Activate Plan", choose your preferred plan and complete the payment instructions. You can also upgrade your plan for higher earnings and more opportunities.

3. When Can I Withdraw My Earnings?

Affiliate Wallet withdrawals are available daily from 5:00 PM to 6:00 PM. Task Wallet withdrawals are available on the 10th and 30th of every month from 4:00 PM to 6:00 PM.

4. What Is The Minimum Withdrawal?

The minimum withdrawal threshold for all Affiliate Wallet is ₦1,000, while the Task Minimum Withdrawal threshold is based on the activated plan. All withdrawals are automatically processed.

5. Why Is My Withdrawal Pending?

Task Wallet withdrawals require manual review before payment to ensure your accuracy and diligence. Affiliate Wallet withdrawals are processed automatically once all requirements are met.

6. How Do Referrals Work?

Share your referral link with others. When they register and activate a plan through your link, you earn a referral bonus based on their selected plan.

7. I Forgot My Password. What Should I Do?

Use the "Forgot Password" option to receive a password reset link provided that you have access to your registered email. Don't forget: Never share your password or login details with anyone including Fortune Earners staff.

8. How Can I Contact Support?

You can contact us through our Telegram support, WhatsApp (when available), or by sending a message using the contact form above.
